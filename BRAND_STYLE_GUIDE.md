# Dr. Abeer Sharara — Brand Style Guide

## Logo

Use `public/dr-abeer-logo.jpg` without altering, recoloring, stretching, or redrawing the monogram. Keep clear space equal to at least one quarter of the visible mark's width. Display it on white or very pale blush surfaces for maximum clarity.

## Color palette

- Primary Magenta: `#C50072` — primary actions, links, icons, and key accents
- Deep Berry: `#830047` — hover states and high-contrast brand surfaces
- Soft Rose: `#F6DCE9` — soft backgrounds and selected states
- Warm Blush: `#FFF7FA` — editorial section background
- Charcoal: `#28242A` — primary text
- Warm Gray: `#6F6870` — secondary text
- White: `#FFFFFF` — primary surface

## Typography

Use Cairo for interface text and Arabic. Use Georgia selectively for large editorial English headings. Headlines are spacious and confident; body text stays clear and restrained.

## Components

- Primary buttons use magenta with white text, a 14px radius, and a soft magenta shadow.
- Secondary links use deep berry with a visible underline or arrow.
- Cards use white surfaces, 22–28px radii, light rose borders, and restrained shadows.
- Inputs use warm white surfaces with rose-gray borders and a clear magenta focus state.

## Spacing and shape

Use an 8px spacing base. Major sections use 80–120px vertical spacing. Curved corners and soft circular motifs should echo the logo without recreating or modifying it.
